More about the company

Most builds get handed down a chain. A salesman signs you, a manager you never meet schedules it, and a rotating cast of subcontractors does the actual work. That is where the quality goes.

High Desert Development runs the other way. the owner is on the job himself, and the trades are in house rather than bid out job to job. The same people who dig your utilities pour your foundation and frame your walls, so nobody is inheriting somebody else's mistakes, and there is one person to ask when you want to know how it is going. He is also a certified home inspector, so the eye on your framing is trained to catch what the final inspection will look for.

More than 30 years of that, across new residential, commercial and private construction, full-service remodeling, and underground utilities. Backyard sports courts too, which is work very few contractors out here take on.

Paying for it

Construction financing is available

A build does not have to wait until the whole amount is sitting in the bank. We offer construction financing on custom homes, ADUs and larger projects, so the conversation can start with what you want to build rather than with what you have saved so far.

Construction loans also tend to work differently from how people expect. Rather than coming due when the build is finished, most are written to roll straight into a regular mortgage on the completed house. How construction financing works lays it out in plain language.

Ask about it when you call and we will walk you through it, with no obligation either way.
