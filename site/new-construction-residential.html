New residential construction in the High Desert

High Desert Development is a California City custom home builder providing new residential construction across the Antelope Valley and the High Desert. We coordinate the major stages of a build, including site preparation, foundations, wood framing, roofing, exterior work, concrete, and detailed interior finishes.

This gallery follows custom homes from foundation slabs and structural framing to finished kitchens, open living spaces, driveways, exterior elevations, and completed desert residences.

Construction financing is available. A build does not have to wait until the full amount is saved. Ask about financing when you call and we will explain how it works, with no obligation.

Rebuilding on a lot where a house already stood is a different set of questions from starting on raw land. Rebuilding a home after a fire goes through them, including the foundation, the permit routes and the contract rules that apply.
