The short version

Privacy policy

This site collects what you type into the contact form, and counts visits. It does not sell anything to anybody, and there is no mailing list.

Plain language, because a policy nobody reads protects nobody. If anything here is unclear, call 760-382-2658 and ask.

What you send us on purpose

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, your phone number, the service you need and whatever you write about the project. That goes straight to High Desert Development by email and nowhere else. It is used to answer you and to quote the work, and it is kept as long as the job and its records need, in the same way a paper enquiry would be.

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, a service that takes the submission and emails it on. It passes through their systems on the way.

Calling or emailing directly reaches the same place, without the form in between.

What gets counted automatically

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are read, roughly where in the world the visitor is, and whether they came from a search, a link or a text message. It records that somebody tapped a phone number or sent the form, not who they are and not what they typed.

Google sets cookies to do that counting. Your browser can block or clear them, and the site works the same either way.

What your own browser remembers

When you open a service page, the site saves the name of that service in your browser so the contact form can preselect it for you. It stays on your device, it is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browsing data removes it.

Things loaded from elsewhere

Two fonts load from Google Fonts, and the walkthrough videos play through Vimeo. Those companies see the request your browser makes to them, which includes your IP address, under their own policies. Nothing is passed to them from the form.

What we do not do

Nothing on this site is sold, rented or traded. There is no advertising, no retargeting pixel, no newsletter and no account to create. We do not ask for payment details anywhere on the website.

Asking for your information back, or gone

Call 760-382-2658 or email highdesertdevelopment@yahoo.com and ask what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted. California residents have that right under state law, and it costs nothing to use it. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

This is a construction company. The site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page. It was last updated in October 2026.
